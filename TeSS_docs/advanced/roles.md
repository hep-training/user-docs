# User roles

Once logged in, you may fit a role in HEP Training that defines your current position more. Having roles in HEP Training allows a user more rights on the platform (e.g., curate materials, curate a space) and/or can lead to credit and recognition of training activities.

## Trainer

*Gives you `visibility`, `credit` and `recognition`.*

If you find yourself providing training, either in-person in your institution or through schools and workshops, or online where you provide step-by-step tutorials or walkthrough videos, you may 'Register as a trainer'.

This will allow you to be publicly featured in the list of [trainers](https://heptraining.cern.ch/trainers) which is useful for workshops' and schools' organisers looking for a specific trainer profile. By having your trainer profile publicly shown, you may have access to both APICURON and BIP! Scholar features which allows to credit and recognize your training contributions. Find more on the [credit and recognition](../advanced/credit-and-recognition) page.

```{admonition} How to be a trainer?
- Register/Log in in <https://heptraining.cern.ch>
- In the [trainers' page](https://heptraining.cern.ch/trainers), click on the 'Register as a trainer' button on the top-right of the window. You are now redirected to the 'Trainers Details' of your user profile.
- Tick the 'Publicly show trainer profile in Trainers page?'
- Fill the relevant information you want to display to the community.
```

## Content Provider Owner/Editor

*Gives you `control` and `visibility` (`credit` and `recognition` to be implemented).*

> Content providers are entities (such as academic institutions, non-profit organisations, portals) that provide training materials of relevance to your community. Every event or training material in HEP Training is associated with precisely one content provider.
Registering your entity as a content provider allows to be featured in the list of ‘Training Providers’ in the main page, this allows your to be included in our growing community of training providers.

From the [Content provider](../accounts/content-provider) page.

Being a Content provider owner or editor allows you to keep your trainings up-to-date in HEP Training by registering resources on behalf of your institution/team/group.

```{admonition} How to be a content provider owner/editor?
- Register/Log in in <https://heptraining.cern.ch>
- For owners: See procedure in the [register section of the content provider page](/content-provider#register)
- For editors: The content provider owner must edit the content provider and add you as an 'Approved editor', see the [edit (point 3) section of the content provider page](/content-provider#edit)
```

## Space Administrator

*Gives you `control`.*

Space administrators have full control over the space they are managing ([What is a space?](/spaces)). This means that all registered resources of a given space, can be edited and curated by its space administrator.

Only private space administrators (and administrators) can curate private space resources ([What is a *private* space?](/spaces#private-space)).

```{admonition} How to be a space administrator?
- If you wish to manage a space that is not yet created, please [request a space](/spaces#request-a-new-space).
- If a space is already set up and you wish to be able to curate and manage its resources, send an email to <contact.heptraining@cern.ch> to explain your motivations. We will get in touch with the space administrator and process your request.
```

## Curator

*Gives you `control` (`credit` and `recognition` to be implemented).*

Curators' goal is to keep HEP Training a platform of high-quality and up-to-date training materials. They can [curate and edit](/curation) all materials from the default and public spaces.

```{admonition} How to be a curator?
[contact.heptraining@cern.ch](mailto:contact.heptraining@cern.ch) to help us as a curator.
```

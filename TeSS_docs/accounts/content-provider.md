# Content Provider

[Content providers](https://heptraining.cern.ch/content_providers) are entities (such as academic institutions, non-profit organisations, portals) that provide training materials of relevance to your community.
Every event or training material in HEP Training is associated with precisely one content provider.

Registering your entity as a content provider allows to be featured in the list of 'Training Providers' in the main page, this allows your to be included in our growing community of training providers.

```{admonition} Each content provider has the following properties:
:class: note
* Title, URL, description, logo, keywords
* One of three types: Organisation, Project, Portal
* One or more approved editors ([HEP Training user accounts](./user)) – To be the owner or editor of a Content provider, ask the administrator
```

## View

1. In the top-right menu, click 'Directory', then click ['Providers'](https://heptraining.cern.ch/content_providers).
2. The list of all visible content providers is shown, with a search box at the top. For each provider, you can see how many events and materials are registered.
3. There are filters on the left to limit the search. There is an option to 'Show hidden items' in the search results.

## Register

Content providers can be created by any registered user. New providers are automatically approved.

1. In the top menu, click 'Directory', then click 'Providers'.
2. Click the buttom 'Register content provider'.
3. Complete the fields (required fields are marked with an asterisk) then click the  'Register Content Provider' button.
4. Once your Content Provider created, you can add a source (['What is a source?'](../content/sources)).

## Update

1. Navigate to the content provider page.
2. Click the 'Edit' button at the top-right.
3. You can add '**Approved editors**' which are registered users who will be able to edit and register resources on behalf of your entity.
4. Make your changes then click the 'Update Content Provider' button.

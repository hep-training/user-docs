# Who can do what?

## For everyone

- Browse training High-Energy Physics training materials
  - [example?](https://heptraining.cern.ch/materials) | [how?](/searching)
- Search training materials and events, for example, python courses from HSF
  - [example?](https://heptraining.cern.ch/materials?content_provider=HEP+Software+Foundation&q=python) | [how?](/searching#browsing-and-filtered-search)
- Subscribe to the next training events and/or add them in your calendar
  - [example?](https://heptraining.cern.ch/events) | [how?](/subscribe)

## For anyone part of a HEP/HEP-related experiment or project

- Browse training resources coming from a specific space, for example, the ones from the EOSC CERN Node
  - [example?](https://heptraining.cern.ch/spaces/1) | [how?](/spaces)

## For anyone involved in providing HEP/HEP-related training, or in education

- Register training materials and events, for example, your hands-on training on C++
  - [example?](https://heptraining.cern.ch/events/15th-hep-c-course-and-hands-on-training-advanced-c) | [how?](/introduction-content)
- Register training materials and events *on behalf of your team/group/institution which provides trainings*, for example the advanced training of ROOT, from the team itself
  - [example?](https://heptraining.cern.ch/materials/root-advanced-course-2026) | [how?](/roles#content-provider-owner-editor)
- Be in a list of trainers and recognized as such for my contributions
  - [example?](https://heptraining.cern.ch/trainers) | [how?](/roles#trainer)
- Help in the curation process of validating training resources
  - [how?](/curation)
- Have an ORCID entry in your profile showing your contributions for training
  - [how?](/credit-and-recognition#apicuron)

## For anyone managing HEP/HEP-related trainings / educative content

- Automatically register training materials to feature them in HEP Training
  - [example?](https://heptraining.cern.ch/content_providers/hsf-training-center) | [how?](/sources)
- Manage a training catalogue for your community
  - [example?](https://heptraining.cern.ch/spaces) | [how?](/roles#space-administrator)
